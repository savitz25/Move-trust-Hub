# NJ-MOVE-001 Public records request — Public Movers & Warehousemen roster

**To:** Records Custodian, New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs / Office of Consumer Protection, Regulated Business Section  
**From:** MoveTrustHub research ingest (internal regulatory evidence)  
**Re:** Machine-readable export of current and historical Public Movers and Warehousemen (PM / PW / PC) authority

## Why this request exists

Current official verification for Public Movers and Warehousemen is the DCA RGB Custom Portal (`https://rgbportal.dca.njoag.gov/public-view/`, also reached via `https://mymoversnj.gov/`).

The MyLicense “Verification_Bulk” business download lists 142 professions and **does not include** Public Movers and Warehousemen. The verification homepage states PMW license lookup has moved to the Custom Portal.

The portal search is not a supported complete statewide export. This request is filed instead of scraping individual search results.

## Preferred format

CSV or XLSX.
