# Maine household-goods regulatory audit

Audit baseline: `origin/main` at `6020ab14f9531e59101dce762e1e9b0c51dabcd2`; official sources checked 2026-10-12.

## Finding

The reviewed current Maine framework does not establish a Maine-specific household-goods mover certificate roster. 29-A M.R.S. §552, “Operating authority license required,” is repealed. This is a finding about the reviewed official framework, not a measured zero license population.

Maine continues to regulate motor-carrier operations in other ways. State BMV materials describe federal USDOT number requirements adopted for applicable intrastate carriers, including statutory exceptions; they separately describe Unified Carrier Registration (UCR) applicability. General motor-carrier compliance, USDOT, UCR, vehicle registration, and federal interstate operating authority must not be represented as a Maine mover-specific license.

The Maine AG consumer page describes federal household-goods consumer protections for interstate moves and states that those federal rules do not apply to moves between two locations in Maine. The page is not evidence of a state mover-license roster, state tariff, or state complaint count. No Maine-address FMCSA rows are used as a state license census.

## Evidence and populations

- Maine HHG-specific intrastate authority roster: not identified in reviewed sources; no state license denominator asserted.
- General carrier/USDOT requirements: separate motor-carrier scope, applicability subject to vehicle and statutory exemptions.
- UCR: separate annual federal-state registration program with stated applicability and exclusions.
- FMCSA interstate authority and USDOT identity: federal and distinct from Maine-specific HHG licensing.
- Maine AG consumer information: consumer guidance, not complaint observations or enforcement findings.
- Insurance, tariffs, and complaints: not acquired as mover-specific state populations.
