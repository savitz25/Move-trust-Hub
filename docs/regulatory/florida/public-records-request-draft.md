**Subject:** Public records request — Chapter 507 mover and moving-broker datasets (machine-readable)

This is a request under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, for electronic copies of records maintained by the Division of Consumer Services relating to Household Moving Services (Chapter 507, F.S.).

Please provide **CSV, XLSX, JSON, or another machine-readable extract** rather than scanned PDFs where that format exists.

## Records requested

1. **Current and historical Chapter 507 intrastate mover registrations** (license type IM / Intrastate Mover), including inactive, expired, suspended, revoked, and denied records if maintained.

2. **Current and historical Chapter 507 moving broker registrations** (license type MB / Moving Broker), including inactive, expired, suspended, revoked, and denied records if maintained.

3. **Broker ↔ registered mover affiliation/relationship records** required by s. 507.03(11), F.S. — the list of registered movers each moving broker has contracted with, is affiliated with, advertises on behalf of, arranges moves for, or refers shippers to. The statute requires FDACS to publish and maintain this list.

4. **Chapter 507 enforcement / administrative-action records**, including notices of noncompliance, administrative complaints, fines, cease-and-desist orders, probation, suspension, revocation, registration denial/refusal, and final orders.

5. **Chapter 507 consumer complaint records and dispositions at the business level** (complaint/case number, received date, category, status, disposition, closed date, whether enforcement resulted). **Do not include consumer names, personal emails, home addresses, or personal telephone numbers.**

6. **Insurance / bond / certificate-of-deposit compliance records** for Chapter 507 registrants if maintained separately from the public license lookup (coverage type, status, effective/expiration/cancellation dates, and any suspension resulting from lapse). Do not include non-public policy documents beyond what is releasable.

7. **Owners, officers, and directors** of Chapter 507 IM and MB registrants, as maintained for the registration file (name, title/role, and any business-file identifier). Do not include personal home addresses or personal telephone numbers.

8. **Registered / resident agents** for Chapter 507 IM and MB registrants (name, business address).
