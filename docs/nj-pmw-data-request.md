# Draft public-record request — New Jersey Public Movers and Warehousemen roster

Status: ready for the product owner to send; **not sent by MoveTrustHub/Codex**.

To: New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs, Regulated Business Section / Records Custodian  
Program: Public Movers and Warehousemen  
Official program contact: `pmw_info@dca.njoag.gov`  
Public records guidance: [New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs OPRA information](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/Pages/OPRA.aspx)

## Request

Please provide the current electronic roster of all Public Movers and Warehousemen license records maintained by the Division, including active, inactive, expired, suspended, revoked, and other publicly releasable statuses.

For each record, please include the following fields if maintained and publicly releasable:

- license number;
- license type, including PM, PW, or PC;
- current status and status-effective date;
- legal/business name;
- DBA, alternate, or trade name;
- physical and mailing addresses, city, state, ZIP, and county;
- public business phone number, email address, and website;
- original issue date and current expiration date;
- responsible party, owner, officer, qualifying individual, registrant, or license-holder fields, preserving the agency's exact relationship terminology;
- publicly releasable disciplinary, enforcement, suspension, revocation, and complaint-history references; and
- any public insurance or bond status fields maintained with the license record.

CSV, XLSX, or JSON is strongly preferred. If no current export exists, please provide the closest machine-readable extract used to populate the anonymous Online Licensee Directory, together with a field/data dictionary. A PDF is requested only if no machine-readable form exists.

This request does not seek Social Security numbers, dates of birth, personal financial information, private contact information, or other exempt information. Publicly presented business and licensing information is sufficient.

Please advise whether the roster is available through an existing download, open-data dataset, documented API, or recurring public-data subscription before creating a new extract.

## Purpose

The information will be used to distinguish official New Jersey mover/warehouseman authority from federal motor-carrier identity and from third-party business information. The project preserves the state record as sourced evidence and does not treat licensure as an endorsement.
