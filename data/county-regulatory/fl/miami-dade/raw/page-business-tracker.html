Quick Start Instructions

  • Enter an Address, Intersection, or Landmark in the search box above to use with the default "Search Settings", and click "Search" to find New/Closed Business Tax Receipts.
  • Optionally, use the "Find By Area" tool in the "Map Tools" menu above to draw an area on the map to search for New/Closed Business Tax Receipts.
  • Modify your search criteria as desired using the "Search Settings" form. You can modify the time range, distance from location, results order, and business classifications filter for the search.
  • The "Search Settings" form can be opened by clicking the "Advanced Search" button above, or the "Modify Search" button in the "Map Tools" menu above.
