Consumer Protection Division

Advocating for the public, we provide assistance with consumer complaints as well as licensing and regulating chauffeurs, taxis and for-hire vehicles; child care providers; emergency and non-emergency medical transport services; motor vehicle repair, body & paint; movers; and towing and immobilization in Broward County.

  2. /Consumer Protection Division

Below is a list of division sections / links found with this website and a description of the information that can be found on these links.

Consumer Affairs:

We issue licenses and permits for several regulated businesses in Broward County. County regulations ensure a fair, safe and competitive marketplace, establish your rights and responsibilities when conducting business with them and also outline how to become a licensee.

Child Care Licensing: 

We improve the quality of child care through regulation and licensing enforcement, including the state-mandated licensing and monitoring of all child care facilities and family child care homes in Broward County.

Regulated Enforcement: 

We are responsible for facilitating enforcement actions for certain consumer protection and environmental programs of Broward County, as regulated by various County Ordinances and State Statutes, to address violations and to compel compliance.

Consumer Complaints: 

We investigate and mediate complaints regarding alleged unfair and deceptive trade practices, as well as violations of County Ordinances. Complaints may be filed online, or by calling 311.

Consumer Affairs Forms: 

All regulated business forms -- including applications, guides and checklists, listed by subject: Chauffeur, Motor Vehicle Repair, Motor Vehicle Body Repair and Paint, Medical Transport, Movers, Towing, and Vehicle-for-Hire forms.

Online Permitting (ePermits OneStop): 

ePermits OneStop allows City customers to submit applications and receive approval for associated Broward County permits and licenses online, accelerating reviews and saving the customer time & money by reducing travel & printing costs.

Broward County Citizen Data Portal: 

Unlocking the Power of Data for Effective Decision-Making and Accountable Government. A single point of access for all things data-related, County-wide.

Frequently Asked Questions: 

Covering Consumer and Environmental related topics.

Consumer Protection Division (CPD) is located at Broward County Governmental Center West (GCW), 1 N. University Drive, Suite 203 Plantation, FL 33324 and is open from 8:00AM - 4:00PM Monday through Friday (excluding holidays).

We regulate Broward County businesses to:

  • Ensure a fair, safe and competitive marketplace
  • Sustain and improve the quality of child care
  • Protect the environment and our water source

Our vision is to be a world-class provider of regulatory services.
Our mission is to protect and improve the quality of life in Broward County through smart regulation. 

Our motto is “We Serve and Protect."
