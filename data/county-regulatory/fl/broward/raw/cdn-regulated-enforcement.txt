Regulated Enforcement 
 
We are responsible for facilitating enforcement actions for certain consumer protection and 
environmental programs of Broward County, as regulated by various County Ordinances and 
State Statutes, to address violations and to compel compliance. 
 
What We Do 
We resolve violations, track, and take action to collect penalties, negotiate settlement 
agreements and restitution, and handle matters that require quasi-judicial administrative or 
judicial hearings.  
 
Contact or Find Us 
For questions, please contact the main line at 954-519-1210 or ECP@broward.org. We are 
located at One N. University Drive, Plantation, Florida 33324. For answers to common 
questions check out our Frequently Asked Questions page.

Government Center West Hearings 
 
Attend and Present Your Case at an Upcoming Hearing 
 
Hearings scheduled at Government Center West (GCW) take place at: 
1 North University Drive  
Hearing Room – Second Floor 
Plantation, Florida 33324 
 
BE AWARE OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
1) Please bring your Notice of Hearing with you to facilitate checking in. 
2) Broward County does not provide translators for legal proceedings. If you feel one is 
needed, you are responsible for providing one, but be aware that some licenses require 
applicants/licensees to be able to read, write, and speak English. 
3) If you have questions, or are exhibiting COVID -like symptoms within 48 hours prior to 
your scheduled hearing, please call the clerk using the phone number listed on your 
Notice of Hearing to reschedule your hearing.

Check the Status of Your Enforcement Case / Contact 
Information 
For questions related to your specific enforcement case, please contact the inspector/case 
manager listed on your Broward County notice; or call the numbers below: 
• Regulated Enforcement - Consumer Protection (954) 765-1700 
• Regulated Enforcement - Environmental Protection (954) 519-1210 
• Regulated Enforcement - Child Care Licensing (954) 357-4800 
 
View Your Enforcement Case Files 
The easiest way to review and check the status of an environmental enforcement action 
(Warning Notice; Citation; Notice of Violation; Notice of Intent to File Suit) is to visit our 
online ENVIROS database: 
• Visit ENVIROS 
• Click on "Enforcement Action" (on the left-hand side) 
• Enter complete case number (e.g., NOV21-0099) 
 
You can also use ENVIROS to search if a facility or location has any pending enforcement 
actions against it, or check for contractor permits/licenses, view outstanding liens, and more!  
 
To schedule an office appointment to review a physical enforcement case file, contact us at 
(954) 519-1210.

Apply for an Environmental Variance 
 
A variance is the authorization to conduct an activity that is contrary to Broward County Code 
(BCC). Variances are granted by hearing examiner  based on criteria found in the Code and 
following a quasi -judicial public hearing. Follow the  instructions on this webpage under 
Regulated Enforcement, Variance Instructions for submitting an application. For questions or 
assistance call (954) 519-1210.

Services Provided by Regulated Enforcement 
 
Regulated Enforcement is responsible for facilitating enforcement actions for certain 
regulated consumer protection and environmental programs of Broward County. Compliance 
with local, state, and federal regulations is our main goal. Broward County's Public Works and 
Environmental Services Department uses enforcement authority to ensure compliance. 
Broward County's authority to enforce regulations is delineated in the Broward County Code 
and Florida Administrative Code. 
 
Our Services Include: 
• Researching, issuing, and tracking enforcement actions such as: 
o Warning Notices 
o Citations 
o Notices of Violation 
o Notices of Intent to File Suit 
• Negotiating settlement agreements and restitution 
• Presenting cases to hearing examiners/boards in quasi-judicial and administrative 
hearings 
• Collecting/acting upon delinquent penalties and outstanding corrective actions 
• Maintaining official county records and serving as clerk during hearings
