Air Permit, County: Application for Air License to operate direct or indirect air pollution source. 
Asbestos: Statement of Responsibilities Regarding Asbestos (SRRA): Certification that building 
permit applicants conducting renovations, demolitions and reroofs have accounted for asbestos 
concerns. 
Building Permits – BMSD (Unincorporated Broward County): Permit to construct, repair, alter, 
move, or demolish any building or structure (including electrical, gas, mechanical and plumbing 
systems). 
Chauffeur Registration Application: Application for Chauffeur License. We regulate chauffeurs that 
drive taxicabs, luxury sedans, stretch limousines, transport vans, and paratransit service vehicles in 
Broward County. 
Construct or Modify a Wastewater Treatment or Reclamation Facility: Application prior to the 
construction, alteration, or operation of a Wastewater Treatment Plant or Reclamation Facility. 
Contaminated Site / EAR Projects: Application for assessment and cleanup of contaminated sites not 
exempt under Chapter 27 of Broward County Code. 
Development & Environmental Review Approval: Prior to Building Permit, Plan review for 
compliance with environmental requirements and plat approval conditions (new construction, interior 
renovation, land clearing). 
Environmental Assessment & Remediation Dewatering: Certified Dewatering Plan review and 
approval for dewatering activity within ¼ mile of a contamination site. 
Environmental Resource License: License for installation of boat slips, impacts to wetlands, docks 
over 500 square feet and dredging or filling of more than 20 cubic yards. 
Environmental Resource License, General & Floating Vessel Platforms: License for installation of 
boat slips, floating vessel platforms, impacts to wetlands, docks under 500 square feet and dredging or 
filling of less than 20 cubic yards. 
Hazardous Material Management Facility License (HMMF License): License required for a 
business that uses, stores, handles, and/or generates twenty-five (25) gallons or more of hazardous 
materials (all total) within a month.

Hazardous Material Transfer Facility License: License for a site or facility that stores or processes 
discarded hazardous material, sludge, or biomedical waste prior to or during transport to a disposal

Marine Facility Operating License (MFOL) Renewal: Renewal of permit for any marina or

Solid Waste Management License: License to construct, operate, modify, or close a Solid Waste 
Management facility. 
Storage Tank Facility License: License required prior to building, erecting, operating, modifying, 
replacing, or closing any storage tank system for storing hazardous materials. 
Surface Water Management: License required prior to activities that alter the flow of surface water 
(i.e. construction of impervious surfaces, paving, grading, and drainage). 
Tree Removal License: Permit required prior to removal or relocation of any trees on designated 
properties in Broward County (see ePermits detail card for list of properties). 
Waste Transporter License: License required for the transport of regulated waste within Broward 
County (Sludges, Discarded Hazardous Materials, Biomedical Waste). Can also be used for 
submission of Monthly Report. 
Wastewater Construction: Permit/license required for construction, extension or alteration of 
wastewater collection/transmission or reuse system (including manhole, gravity sewer main, force 
main, pump station, vacuum sewer system). 
Wastewater Treatment Plant Operating License: All wastewater treatment plants are required to 
submit their operating license renewal applications by the 1st day of May of every year. 
Water & Wastewater Services Utility Connection Permit: Four-part permit process that allows 
connection to or modification of a potable water, wastewater and/or reclaimed water system.

Permit 
Required For 
The SRRA helps to ensure that building owners/operators and permit applicants 
properly account for any asbestos concerns before start of a building reroofing, 
renovating or demolition project. Reroofing, Demolition, Renovation of 
Commercial or Residential Buildings in Broward County. 
  
Permit Process REVIEW TIME FOR SRRA SUBMITTALS DEEMED COMPLETE. 
“Deemed Complete” means that all required documentation has been satisfactorily 
reviewed, including the scope of work, asbestos survey, notice of renovation or 
demolition, and payment of all applicable fees. 
• Up to 1 week for residential buildings with four or fewer dwelling units, 
including single-family homes, duplexes, triplexes, and quadplexes 
used exclusively for residential purposes. 
• Up to 2 weeks for all other projects, including commercial and industrial 
facilities, condominiums, and townhouses with five or more attached residential 
dwelling units. 
  
Tips to Avoid 
Delays 
• Pay applicable fees.  
• Submit scope of work or demo plans (do not upload or submit architectural, 
electrical, plumbing, etc. plans). 
• Submit asbestos survey performed or supervised by a Florida Licensed 
Asbestos Consultant. 
• Submit Notice of Demolition or Asbestos Renovation for all building 
demolitions or asbestos renovation projects with 160 sq. ft. or more (1% or 
more asbestos fibers content).  
• Submit asbestos survey performed or supervised by a Florida Licensed 
Asbestos Consultant for all built-up roofing low-slope (aka flat) Reroofing 
jobs of 5,580 sq. ft. or more. 
• If after the initial review, updates are made in ePermits or requested 
documentation has been emailed, please email us 
at AsbestosHelp@broward.org (please include ePermits number in the subject 
line).

Other 
Requirements 
Notice: An Environmental Review Approval is required prior to the issuance of a 
building permit by the County. Any work not considered NEW and which 
involves the repairing, replacing, or altering of an existing structure such as a Re-
Roof, will require Asbestos Review as well. Driveways will require Engineering 
approval. 
  
Permit 
Required For 
A Building Permit is required to construct, enlarge, alter, repair, move, remove, 
demolish, or to change the occupancy of any building or structure. A Building 
Permit is also required to erect, install, enlarge, alter, repair, remove, convert, or 
replace any impact-resistant coverings, and electrical, gas, mechanical, or 
plumbing systems. 
  
Permit Process Other agency approvals such as DER, Asbestos and Engineering, as may be 
required, must be obtained prior to applying for a Building Permit. Florida 
Building Code allows for a 30-business day review of residential permit 
applications, however, reviews are normally performed and approved, or 
comments are issued within 15 business days or sooner. Follow this link for 
application review status Permit Status. 
  
Tips to Avoid 
Delays 
• Make sure Development and Environmental Review has been completed and 
submitted with Application.  
• When Physical plans are required, they must be submitted with proper 
signatures. Electronic/digital signatures from engineers and architects on 
physically submitted drawings are not permitted per board rules.  
• Make sure a job value is shown on the building permit application. 
• General Liability and Workers Compensation must be current prior to the 
issuance of permit(s).

ePermits User Guide 
  
Other 
Requirements 
Additional Broward County approvals/licenses may be required depending on the 
scope of work for the proposed project.  Once the initial review of the project is 
complete, staff will inform the applicant of additional approvals/licenses that may 
be required. 
  
Permit 
Required For 
This countywide activity takes place prior to the issuance of a building permit by 
the county or any municipal building department. Plans are reviewed for 
compliance with all environmental requirements and plat approval conditions. 
Additionally, education fees may be assessed if required. This approval is required 
as a prerequisite for all the building permits and includes new construction, 
additions, interior renovations and land clearing. Please note that ALL projects 
may only be submitted electronically. Walk-in plan reviews are no longer 
available. 
  
Permit 
Process 
The steps for a Development and Environmental Review are  
•    Visit the ePermits website 
•    If this is your first time you will need to register an account. 
•    Complete an application for Development and Environmental Review and 
upload your plans.

ePermits OneStop 
ePermits One Stop 
Broward.org/ePermits 
•    Receive your confirmation email and await an email 
from DERPlans@broward.org 
•    Respond to any inquires from the email, if no inquires pay the associated 
fees. Beginning November 20, 2025, Broward County will no longer collect 
Education Fees.  Education Fees will be collected by the School Board of 
Broward County only.  For more information regarding payment of Education 
Fees, please call 754-321-1980 or 
email treasury@browardschools.com.  Environmental Review fees MUST still 
be paid to the County. Fees can only be paid online via the e-permits website. 
E-check only for Development Review and E-check or credit card for 
Environmental Review. 
•    Print your two certificates (Transportation Concurrency & Environmental). If 
the project has education fees, the    School Board will send a payment link to 
the email address on the application.  Pay the education fees and receive a 
payment confirmation from the School Board.  Failure to pay Education fees to 
the School Board will result in a revocation/cancellation of your approval 
certificates.  After outstanding fees have been satisfied, take/submit the 3 items 
(Development Review AKA Transportation Concurrency Satisfaction 
Certificate, School Board payment receipt and Environmental Review Approval 
Certificate) to the city building department within 90 calendar days or the 
certificates will expire. 
  
Tips to Avoid 
Delays 
• Have a complete and accurate address on plans. 
• Have a complete set of plans. This includes but not limited to architectural, 
structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, civil and survey plans. 
• Provide a scope of work.  
• List only the name and email address of the person who will be responsible for 
the review and approval process of the submittal. 
• Submit plans in pdf format only. 
• Group or block upload plans.  Do not upload pages individually.   
• Look for emails from DERPlans@broward.org

ePermits User Guide 
  
Other 
Requirements 
An Environmental Review Approval is required prior to the issuance of a building 
permit by the County or any municipal building department. Additionally, a 
permit for dewatering may be required by the Florida Department of 
Environmental Protection, or the South Florida Water Management District. 
  
Permit 
Required For 
Approval is required to conduct dewatering activities within 1/4 mile radius of a 
contaminated site. It is recommended that applicants should first investigate 
the Contaminated Sites Interactive Map (to see if the proposed dewatering is 
within 1/4 mile of a contaminated site).  
  
Permit Process A $400 review fee is required to obtain a Dewatering Plan Approval. Upload the 
certified Dewatering Plan to the ePermits website.  Once the Dewatering Plan is 
accepted, payment of the review fee will be requested.  Reviews are issued within 
10 business days.  
 
Tips to Avoid 
Delays 
• Please make sure that a representative of the entity that is assuming 
responsibility for the dewatering project is clearly named in the Dewatering

• The Plan must provide details on the proposed controls and monitoring

information provided by the applicant. All applications will be reviewed within 30 days of
