Consumer Complaints 
We investigate and mediate complaints from consumers regarding alleged unfair and 
deceptive trade practices as well as violations of County Ordinances. Complaints may be filed 
online, or by calling 311. 
 
What We Do 
• We administer, regulate and enforce county, state and federal consumer protection laws 
and rules involving unfair or deceptive trade practices and offer information and referrals 
involving consumer issues. 
• We will investigate and mediate your complaint if you feel you are a victim of an unfair 
or deceptive trade practice by a business located in Broward County or involving a 
service performed in Broward County. 
• We also issue citations against a variety of businesses, including chauffeurs, taxis, and 
for-hire vehicles; emergency and non -emergency medical transportation providers; 
motor vehicle repair , body & paint shops and technicians; movers ; and towing and 
Immobilization companies. 
 
File a Complaint 
We accept complaints from consumers regarding alleged unfair and deceptive trade practices 
of businesses, generally limited to those businesses which are located in Broward County.  If 
you believe you have been a victim of an unfair and/or deceptive trade practice, please 
file a  complaint online . Alternatively, you may  call Broward  311 to file a complaint 
or with any questions. 
 
When filing a complaint, please explain your problem simply and concisely. Be sure to include 
copies of all documents related to your complaint, including but not limited to Receipts, 
Contracts, Pictures, Proof of Payment, Advertisements and Correspondence. 
Remember: send only copies – keep your originals!

Other Complaint Resources 
If we are unable to handle your complaint and we know of another enforcement agency which 
may be able to assist you, we will provide you with referral information. If you have a question 
about a consumer issue other than a complaint, we will provide you with any available resources 
and information we may have. Some complaints, however, are best resolved in a court of law.  
 
Here are some of the other enforcement agencies that may be able to help: 
Federal Trade Commission 
• Complaints involving unfair, deceptive, or fraudulent practices 
Broward County Clerk of the Courts 
• Small claims cases (up to $5,000) 
Broward County Clerk of the Courts 
• Landlord/Tenant disputes 
Florida Division of Hotels and Restaurants 
• Hotel and restaurant complaints 
Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration 
• Interstate moves of household goods involving a moving company, moving broker, or 
auto transporter 
Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation 
• Complaints against business professionals licensed by the state of Florida (for example, 
contractors, real estate agents, etc.)
