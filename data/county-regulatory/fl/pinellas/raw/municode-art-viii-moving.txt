CREDENTIAL FINDING:
No section in Article VIII creates a county mover license, registration, permit, or roster credential.
Regulation is behavioral/transactional (estimates, contracts, disclosures, inventory, liability, enforcement).
Official Consumer Protection website lists Moving under Monitoring, not Licensing.
