# Rhode Island household-goods authority evidence

Primary regulator: Rhode Island Division of Public Utilities and Carriers (DPUC), Motor Carriers Division.

- Moving information and licensed mover list: https://ripuc.ri.gov/utility-information/motor-carriers/moving-information
- Motor carrier division jurisdiction: https://ripuc.ri.gov/utility-information/motor-carriers/responsibilities-and-jurisdiction-motor-carriers-division
- Motor carrier forms (including household-goods bill of lading, tariff, and insurance-related forms): https://ripuc.ri.gov/utility-information/motor-carriers/motor-carrier-forms
- FMCSA SAFER (federal interstate records only): https://safer.fmcsa.dot.gov/

The attached official licensed-movers PDF is dated December 17, 2025. It has 43 carrier entries and 43 distinct MC certificate identifiers. The PDF is a dated snapshot, not a real-time roster; current certificate status was not independently established. Do not treat MC identifiers as USDOT numbers or equate the DPUC intrastate certificate with FMCSA interstate authority.

Retrieved October 14, 2026. SHA-256: `files-sha256.csv`.

Other carriers, applications, tariffs, insurance filings, annual reports, complaints, and orders remain separate evidence grains. A tariff or insurance filing requirement does not prove current authority or coverage; a complaint is not a finding.
